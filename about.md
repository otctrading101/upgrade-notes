Upgrade Notes is a weekly digest of what changed in AI for builders: what shipped, what it breaks, and what to do about it. Every news item comes from a primary or reputable source that we link. Spot an error? [Open an issue](https://github.com/otctrading101/upgrade-notes/issues) and we'll correct it in the next issue.

From the team behind [ShipRules](https://shiprules.gumroad.com), AI coding rules for Cursor, Claude Code and Copilot.
